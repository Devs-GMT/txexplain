## Why not every post tags @BOTChain_ai

- The brief asks for the tag on the demo post, so that one always has it.
- Posts 1 and 3 name BOT Chain facts (the chain's failures, the mainnet contract), so a tag is relevant.
- Posts 2 and 4 are explanations that stand alone. Tagging them adds nothing and reads as spam, which makes the account easier to mute.
- The account is a community tool, not an official one, and the bio says so.

## Schedule and rules

- One post a day, the video last.
- Every figure in an image comes from the live app. Example transactions are labelled "Example".
- The images are made from screenshots of the live site and kept out of the repo. The video is built separately.
